# E-Commerce Data Analytics Project 📊

An end-to-end **E-Commerce Data Analytics project** focused on analyzing sales performance, customer segments, product performance, and business trends to generate meaningful and actionable insights.

This project was completed as part of my **first Data Analytics internship with Camera Solutions**, providing practical exposure to working with real-world-style business data and transforming raw data into useful business insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to analyze an e-commerce dataset and understand key business areas such as:

* Overall sales performance
* Monthly sales trends
* Top-performing products
* Customer segment performance
* Product-level performance
* Regional/business performance
* Opportunities for improving sales and profitability

The analysis combines **data cleaning, SQL querying, Excel analysis, and Power BI visualization** to create a complete data analytics workflow.

## 📊 Key Analysis Areas

### 1. Sales Performance

The project analyzes overall sales performance to identify products and customer groups that contribute significantly to revenue.

### 2. Product Analysis

Top-performing products were identified to understand which products contribute most strongly to sales.

These products can be prioritized for:

* Inventory availability
* Promotions
* Targeted marketing
* Sales strategy

### 3. Monthly Sales Trends

Monthly sales analysis was performed to identify periods of higher and lower sales.

This can support:

* Inventory planning
* Promotional campaigns
* Seasonal planning
* Sales forecasting

### 4. Customer Segmentation

Different customer segments were analyzed to understand their contribution to overall revenue.

Higher-value customer segments can be targeted through:

* Personalized offers
* Retention strategies
* Targeted marketing campaigns

### 5. Product Strategy

Lower-performing products can be reviewed based on sales volume and customer demand.

Possible business actions include:

* Improving promotions
* Reviewing pricing
* Improving product positioning
* Understanding customer demand

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis highlights several important business opportunities:

* A relatively small group of products contributes significantly to overall sales.
* High-performing products should receive greater attention in inventory and marketing decisions.
* Monthly sales trends can help businesses plan inventory and promotional activities more effectively.
* Customer segments contribute differently to revenue, creating opportunities for targeted customer strategies.
* Low-performing products should be evaluated to determine whether pricing, promotion, or product positioning needs improvement.
* Combining product, customer, and time-based analysis can support better data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, businesses can consider:

1. Prioritizing high-performing products for inventory and promotional campaigns.
2. Focusing marketing efforts on high-value customer segments.
3. Using monthly sales trends for better inventory planning.
4. Reviewing low-performing products and evaluating their pricing and promotion strategies.
5. Using data-driven customer targeting and retention strategies.
6. Combining multiple business dimensions to make more informed decisions.
